Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

At Vitality74, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you use Vitality74, you may provide the following information:

  • Profile information (age range, fitness level, goals)
  • Training session data (workout completion, duration, performance ratings)
  • Nutrition tracking data (food logs, hydration)
  • Health-related self-assessments

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics (features used, session frequency)
  • Crash reports and performance data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide personalized training recommendations
  • Track your progress and display statistics
  • Send notifications and reminders (if enabled)
  • Improve the App's functionality and user experience
  • Generate anonymous, aggregated analytics

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All personal health and training data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your personal training data, performance logs, or health information to external servers. Your journey is private and stays on your device.

3.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data, including:

  • Local data encryption using device-level security
  • No cloud storage of personal health data
  • No user accounts or authentication required

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share anonymous, aggregated data for:

  • Analytics and app improvement
  • Research purposes (fully anonymized)

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Expo/React Native: App framework and push notifications
  • Analytics services: Anonymous usage statistics only

These services have their own privacy policies governing data they collect.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data stored by the App
  • Delete: Remove all your data by uninstalling the App or using the "Clear Data" option in Settings
  • Opt-out: Disable notifications and reminders at any time
  • Export: Request a copy of your data

7. Children's Privacy

Vitality74 is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@vitality74.app
  • Support: Visit our Support page

10. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Since we store all data locally on your device and do not sell personal information, most CCPA provisions do not apply. However, you may still contact us with any privacy-related questions.

11. International Users

If you are accessing the App from outside the United States, please be aware that your data remains on your device and is not transferred internationally. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with GDPR requirements by design through our local-only data storage approach.
