Getting Started

When you first open the app, you'll be guided through a quick onboarding process. Answer questions about your age, fitness level, experience with pelvic floor exercises, and your goals. This helps us create a personalized 74-day training plan tailored to your starting point.

Before your first workout, complete the Calibration Wizard to learn proper technique. This 3-step process teaches you how to locate, isolate, and control your PC muscle using the "elevator" concept. Proper form is essential for results and safety.

Before each session, the Pre-Flight Checklist reminds you to empty your bladder and reset your posture. These simple steps ensure you get the most out of each training session and can focus entirely on your workout.

Follow along with the visual biofeedback circle and haptic cues. The breathing animation guides you through each contraction and relaxation phase. Cyan means relax, amber/orange means squeeze. Focus on isolating your PC muscle - keep your abs, thighs, and glutes relaxed.

After each session, log your performance metrics in the Progress tab. Rate your stamina, control, and duration. Over time, you'll see your scores improve and can track your journey with detailed bio-analytics and trend visualizations.
